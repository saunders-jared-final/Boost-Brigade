UI Pod Wiring

How the LCD, temperature probe, IR receiver and RGB LED connect to the Raspberry Pi. The mini breadboard carries only the shared connections: the 3.3 V and ground buses, the probe's pull-up resistor and the level converter for the LCD. The IR receiver and RGB module wire straight to the Pi.

Wire everything with the Pi unplugged

No 5 V wire may ever touch a GPIO pin. On this layout, 5 V exists only in column 8 of the bottom half, the LCD, and Pi pin 2.

Mini breadboard

Each column's five holes a–e are joined, and so are f–j. The two halves are separate, and this board has no power rails. Numbered circles are wire ends; the table below says where each goes.

3.3 V 5 V Ground SDA SCL Probe data + − D probe leads, in their own colours
  • The converter straddles the centre gap with its 3.3 V (LV) pins in the top half and its 5 V (HV) pins in the bottom half. Its pins may land in rows other than e and f; only the columns matter.
  • The pin order drawn is the common one (1, 2, LV/HV, GND, 3, 4). If your board's labels differ, follow the labels: each wire goes to the column of the pin named in the table.
  • Channels 3 and 4 (columns 10 and 11) are spare.

Raspberry Pi header

Pin 1 is at the end of the header nearest the microSD card, on the inner row. Its solder joint on the underside is square. Odd pins run along the inner row, even pins along the board edge.

Before you power on

  1. Converter supplies. LV (column 8, top) goes to 3.3 V and HV (column 8, bottom) goes to 5 V. Swapping them puts 5 V on the Pi's I²C pins.
  2. RGB module type. The design assumes the shared pin is marked − (common cathode). If it's marked + or V, wire that pin to 3.3 V instead of ground, and the colours will be inverted in software (on is low).
  3. Probe lead colours. Red, black and yellow is the usual scheme, but check the packet. Tin the stranded ends with solder so they push into the breadboard cleanly.
  4. Loose ends. No bare lead or resistor leg should touch a neighbouring column.

Turn the interfaces on

Add these lines to the end of /boot/firmware/config.txt on the Pi, then reboot.

dtparam=i2c_arm=on
dtoverlay=w1-gpio,gpiopin=4
dtoverlay=gpio-ir,gpio_pin=17

LCD

sudo apt install -y i2c-tools
i2cdetect -y 1

Expect 27 or 3f in the grid; that's the LCD's address. If the screen lights but shows nothing, or only solid blocks, turn the small contrast screw on the backpack.

Temperature probe

cat /sys/bus/w1/devices/28-*/temperature

Prints the heatsink temperature in thousandths of a degree, for example 24312 for 24.3 °C.

IR receiver

sudo apt install -y ir-keytable
sudo ir-keytable -c -p all -t

Press buttons on the remote; each press prints a scancode. Ctrl+C to stop.

RGB LED

python3 -c "from gpiozero import RGBLED; from time import sleep
led = RGBLED(12, 13, 19)
for c in [(1,0,0),(0,1,0),(0,0,1)]:
    led.color = c; sleep(1)"

Shows red, green, then blue for a second each. If the order is wrong, swap wires 16 to 18.
